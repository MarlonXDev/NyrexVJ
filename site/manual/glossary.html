#Terms

These are the words this manual uses for parts of the program and for show terms, in alphabetical order.

TermMeaning
BlackoutTurns the final picture black at once, key B
Blend modeHow a layer mixes with the layers below it
BypassSkips a layer or an effect as if it were not there
CellOne place in the clip grid that holds a clip
ClipSomething that draws a picture: a generative clip, a video, an image, a model, a shader or a live source
ColumnThe eight cells of the same number across all layers, triggered together
CompositionEverything of a show: decks, layers, effects, colours and settings
CueAn action that fires at a time of the timecode
DeckA complete set of four layers and eight columns, there are 16
EffectA change that is applied to the picture of a layer or the master
GateA mode in which an effect shows only while a band is loud
ISFInteractive Shader Format, a shader file format with a description header
KickA short pulse that the analysis finds on the bass beats
LayerOne of four levels of the picture, higher layers lie over lower ones
LFOA low frequency oscillator, a movement that repeats over a number of beats
LTCLinear timecode, timecode that comes as an audio signal
MacroA knob that can drive many values at once
MasterThe final picture and the controls that work on all of it
MTCMIDI timecode
NDIA way to send a picture over the network
PresetA saved look with all 16 decks
SessionThe state of the program as you left it, restored on the next start
SliceA part of an output with its own picture area, warp, mask and colour
SpoutA way to share a picture between programs on Windows
Tap tempoSetting the tempo by tapping a key in time with the music
Venue setupThe saved output and mapping settings of a room
VRSLVR Stage Lighting, which reads DMX data from a strip in the video
