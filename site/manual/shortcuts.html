Keyboard shortcuts and mouse

Chapter 31 of 37 · 1 min read

On this pageKeyboardMouse

#Keyboard

These are the keys as delivered. Most of them can be changed in Settings, Shortcuts (see Interface, workspaces and accessibility). They work everywhere in the main window, except while you type into a text field.

KeyAction
1 to 8Trigger a column
SpaceTap tempo
BBlackout
Page Up and Page DownPrevious and next used deck
Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8Deck 1 to 8
Ctrl+Shift+1 to Ctrl+Shift+8Deck 9 to 16
Ctrl+ZUndo
Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+ZRedo
Ctrl+,Open Settings
Ctrl+Shift+RStart or stop recording
Ctrl+=, Ctrl+-, Ctrl+0Larger interface, smaller interface, normal interface size
F1Open or close this manual
F11Fullscreen for the output windows
EscLeave fullscreen

The keys do nothing while you type in a text field. In the timeline window Space plays and pauses, and Home jumps to the start.

#Mouse

ActionResult
Click a cellPlay that clip on its layer
Ctrl+click a cellShow it in the Preview without playing
Click a column headerTrigger the whole column
Right click a cellAssign or clear a clip, or preview
Right click a deck buttonRename, copy, fill, clear or remove the deck
Right click a sliderReset it to its default
Click a clip in the LibraryPut it into the selected cell and play it
Drag from the Library to a cellAssign the clip
Click or drag an effect in the LibraryAdd it to the selected layer
B, S and X on a layerBypass, solo and stop the layer
