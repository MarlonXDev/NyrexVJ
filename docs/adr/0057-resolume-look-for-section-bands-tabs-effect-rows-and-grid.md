# ADR 0057: Resolume look for the section bands, the dock tabs, the effect rows and the clip grid

- **Status:** Accepted, requested by MarlonXDev after 0.62.0 (2026-11-27 16:11): the interface still did not look "1 to 1" like Resolume Arena, in the overlay, layout, look, design and style. A second message the same evening (17:59) added three findings that are handled in points 6 to 8: the windows have no X to close them, a Dear ImGui warning about two buttons shows up, and Transition and Auto cycle "are gone". It reverses point 5 of [ADR 0055](0055-resolume-look-for-every-colour-and-no-window-close-buttons.md). Issue [#144](https://github.com/MarlonXDev/NyrexVJ/issues/144).
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Owners:** Maintainers (pending assignment)
- **Related:** [ADR 0055](0055-resolume-look-for-every-colour-and-no-window-close-buttons.md), [ADR 0053](0053-window-layout-transport-bar-and-docking-timeline.md), [INTERFACES.md](../INTERFACES.md), [TESTING.md](../TESTING.md), [DEPENDENCIES.md](../DEPENDENCIES.md)

## Context
- The Resolume Arena 7.3.3 screenshot from 0.61.0 was compared with the program part by part at four times the size. The differences that were left: the sections of a panel were rounded Dear ImGui bars with a large arrow, the drop-down lists had a boxed arrow button, the tabs were left aligned and as wide as their text, the effects were a tick box and a list on the plain panel colour, the tiles of the clip grid sat 5 pixels apart sideways and 17 pixels apart vertically (Resolume: about 3 pixels), and the layer block under the grid was much taller than a row of tiles.
- Measured in the screenshot: the video effect rows are a band of a dark teal (about `#2A5244`) with lighter cells (about `#517065`) for the buttons, the audio effect rows are mauve (`#583D4A`), the tab labels are centred, the tile pitch is about 55 pixels with 3 pixels between the tiles in a window 1069 pixels wide.
- The second message: the tool windows (manual, Settings and others) have no X at the top right to close them; a screenshot shows the Dear ImGui message "2 visible items with conflicting ID" with the plus button of the deck tabs and the plus button of the tempo outlined in red; and the screenshot of an old version has a Transition list, Beat sync and Auto cycle in the deck bar that the new one does not show. They were moved to the Trigger window in 0.60.0, next to the Clip tab, but nothing in the deck bar points there.

## Decision
1. **Section bands.** `ui::collapsingHeader` replaces `ImGui::CollapsingHeader` in all interface files. With the flat look it draws a band over the full width of the panel (`#2C2C2C`, a little lighter when hovered) with a small triangle (down when open, right when closed) and the name close to the left; it keeps the open state of Dear ImGui, so default open, the saved state and `SetNextItemOpen` work as before. With high contrast the old header is used.
2. **Drop-down lists.** `ui::beginCombo` replaces `ImGui::BeginCombo` in all interface files. With the flat look the boxed arrow button is off and a small triangle is drawn at the right edge of the field. A call that already passes `NoArrowButton` or `NoPreview` is left alone.
3. **Effect rows.** In the flat look an effect slot is a band over the full width: a triangle that folds the slot, the list with the effect name, the button B (bypass, lights up in the accent when the effect is bypassed, the sliders turn grey) and the button X (empties the slot and takes the bypass off). The colour of the band is derived from the dim accent of the theme (`kAccentDim * 0.62 + 0.05`, the cells 0.09 lighter, the hovered cells 0.17), so it follows the colour the user chose; in Arena it is a dark teal. The old tick box is kept for high contrast. Whether a slot is folded is kept by Dear ImGui for the session only, not saved.
4. **Clip grid.** The cell padding of the table is 1.5 points in the flat look, the columns are exactly as wide as the tiles (108), so the tile pitch is 111 and the tiles sit 3 points apart, and the rows are as high as a tile (61, pitch 64). The layer block is a tile of the same size with the picture of the layer and the layer name on a bar at the bottom (the bar is mint when the layer is selected), next to it the buttons X, B, S, the blend mode and the opacity. The layer column is 270 wide, was 300.
5. **Tabs.** In the flat look every tab is at least 72 points wide (`TabMinWidthBase`, scaled with the interface size, 0 with high contrast) and the label is centred in its tab. Dear ImGui 1.93 has no setting for the centring, so **one line of `imgui_widgets.cpp` is changed**: the clip rectangle of the label in `TabItemLabelAndCloseButton` starts half of the free width further right. CMake does this once after the download (`string(FIND)` and `string(REPLACE)` on that single line, no patch file, so line endings do not matter and a second configure changes nothing). If the line is not found, for example after a change of the Dear ImGui version, configuring stops with an error that names this ADR. The test `nvj_imgui_tab_patch` checks that the change is in the file.
6. **The X is back on the tool windows.** `ui::beginTool` hands the open flag to `ImGui::Begin` again, so the tool windows (Settings, Creator, Manual, Shader editor, Timeline, Failover, Generators, Nodes, Packs, Routing, ResoPort, Show) show a small X at the right of the title bar, and a click on it closes the window as before. Escape still closes the focused one. The X is drawn by Dear ImGui as a cross in the text colour (checked at four times the size in the Arena colours; the grey square of 0.60.0 was not reproduced). ADR 0055 point 5 is reversed.
7. **Unique identifiers.** The plus button of the deck tabs is `+##newDeck` and the buttons of the tempo are `-##bpmDown` and `+##bpmUp`, so no two buttons of the deck window share an identifier. The Dear ImGui warning for such a conflict (`ConfigDebugHighlightIdConflicts`) is switched on only in `--interface-uitest`, where it is checked by moving the mouse along the deck bar and the transport bar, and off for users, who would only see a programmer message.
8. **Transition, Beat sync and Auto cycle in the transport bar.** When the deck window is wide enough (960 points for the list, 1180 for the two tick boxes) the transport bar shows the Transition list, Beat sync and Auto cycle after the phase display. They change the same deck settings as the Trigger window, which keeps the length and the cycle length sliders. A narrow window shows less of them, as it does with the other controls of the bar.

## Not done
- Equal width tabs that fill the whole tab bar (the tab bar of the right column has six tabs and would need scroll arrows even sooner), a panel title bar in the middle of the window instead of tabs, the audio effect rows in mauve (there are no audio effects), the effect rows as a drag-and-drop list that can be re-ordered, saving which effect rows are folded, Resolume's own font, icons, the Dashboard knobs, the M, A and V buttons, the T and Alph buttons, the crossfader row and a third layer row (as in ADR 0055).
- No change of what any control does, only of how it looks, except the new X and B buttons that do what the old tick box and the empty entry of the list did.
- The length of a transition and the cycle length are not repeated in the transport bar (they stay in the Trigger window), and a hint in the deck bar that points there.

## Consequences
- The program now changes a third-party source file at configure time. It is one line, it is documented here and in `DEPENDENCIES.md`, it is guarded by a test and by the error on a mismatch, and it makes the build depend on the exact text of that line in Dear ImGui 1.93.0.
- The clip grid shows more tiles in the same space; a window that was tuned to the old row height keeps its size and shows empty space under the grid.
- Folded effect slots hide their error messages and notes too; the slots are open by default.
- Checked on a virtual screen (Xvfb, software OpenGL) with screenshots and mouse clicks only, not on a real screen, not on Windows and not with display scaling other than 100 percent; see `M-UI-11`.
